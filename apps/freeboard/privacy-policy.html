Privacy Policy - Freeboard

Last updated: October 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Freeboard ("the App") handles information when you use the App. Freeboard is a whiteboard and notebook app, and everything you create in it stays under your control.

Information You Add

The App stores the boards and notebooks you create, including drawings, handwriting, shapes, stickies, text, scenes, pages, and their names. The App also keeps your preferences, such as tool settings, the toolbar layout, and the canvas theme. This information is used only to show and edit your boards and notebooks.

Storage and iCloud Sync

Your boards and notebooks are stored on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud and iCloud is turned on for the App, they are also stored in your private iCloud database through Apple CloudKit and synchronized across your devices signed in to the same Apple Account. Apple processes this data under its own privacy policy and security practices. The App does not require an account with the developer, and the developer cannot access your boards, notebooks, or iCloud data. You can turn off iCloud for the App in your device settings.

Handwriting Recognition

When you convert handwriting to text, recognition runs on your device with Apple's Vision framework. Your handwriting is not sent to the developer or to any server.

Exported Files

PDF files are created only at your request and leave the App only when you choose a destination through the system sharing interface or save them to Files. You are responsible for protecting files after sharing them.

Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

Freeboard does not include advertising or analytics SDKs, does not collect personal data, does not track you, and does not sell or share data. Apple may provide crash or diagnostic information if you have chosen to share it with developers in your device settings.

Data Retention and Deletion

Data remains on your device until you delete it or remove the App. Data synchronized through iCloud remains in your private iCloud database until you delete it in the App or remove the App's data from iCloud in your device settings.

Children's Privacy

Freeboard does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you have questions about a child's use of the App, please contact us.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact: support@nesporov.dev
