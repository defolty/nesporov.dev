FAQ

Where are my boards stored?
Freeboard stores boards and notebooks on your device and, when iCloud is on for the App, in your private iCloud. The developer has no access to them.
Why don't my boards appear on my other devices?
Make sure all devices are signed in to the same Apple Account and that iCloud is turned on for Freeboard in iCloud settings. The first sync can take a few minutes, especially for large boards.
Can I draw on iPhone or Mac?
Drawing and handwriting are on iPad. On iPhone and Mac you can browse boards and notebooks, move between scenes and pages, and add or move stickies.
How do I erase by crossing out?
Scribble over a stroke with a zigzag. Choose whether it erases instantly or when you hold the pen in the tool's Smoothing settings.
How do I export a notebook?
Open the notebook, tap Export, choose the pages and options, then share the PDF or save it to Files. Sections become PDF bookmarks.
How do I change the app language?
Open Freeboard's settings and tap Language. On iPhone and iPad this opens the app's page in Settings; on Mac, Language & Region.
How can I report a bug?
Email the device model, system version, app version, and the steps that reproduce the issue. A screenshot or a screen recording helps a lot.
