Privacy Policy - Alias

Last updated: September 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Alias ("the App") handles information. Alias is a party word game where teams explain words without saying them, played by passing one phone around. The App is designed not to collect any data about you.

Information We Collect

None. The App does not collect, transmit, or sell personal data. It has no analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party SDKs, and it does not require registration or an account.

Information Stored on Your Device

To make the game convenient, the following is saved only on your device:

  • Team names and player names you enter.
  • Game settings such as turn length, points to win, and selected word packs.
  • An unfinished game, so you can continue it later.
  • Game statistics: number of games, guessed and skipped words, team wins, and the best explainer.
  • Word packs you create yourself.

This data is stored locally, is never sent anywhere, and is removed when you delete the App. All word packs are bundled with the App, and the game works without an internet connection.

Purchases

Alias Pro purchases (a subscription and a one-time purchase) are processed by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive or store your payment details. The App only checks with Apple whether your purchase is active. Apple's handling of payments is described in the Apple Privacy Policy.

Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

Alias does not track you across apps or websites, does not use the advertising identifier, and does not show ads. Apple may provide crash or diagnostic information if you have chosen to share it with developers in your device settings.

Data Retention and Deletion

You can reset statistics in Settings, delete an unfinished game from the home screen, delete your own word packs in the pack editor, and remove teams and players during game setup. Deleting the App removes all of its data from your device.

Children's Privacy

Alias is suitable for family play and does not collect data from anyone, including children. The "After Midnight" word pack is intended for adults only (18+).

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact: support@nesporov.dev
