Support - Alias

Party word game for teams: explain the word without saying it, all on one phone.

Support email: support@nesporov.dev

How to Play

  • Split into 2–6 teams. On each turn, one player explains words to their team; the explainer rotates every turn.
  • You cannot say the word itself, words with the same root, translate it, or spell it. Synonyms, antonyms, and descriptions are allowed.
  • Swipe up when a word is guessed and swipe left to skip it. The bubble shows how much time is left.
  • Each guessed word gives +1 point. If the skip penalty is on, each skip costs 1 point. You can correct results after the turn.
  • The word on screen when time runs out is the last word. With "last word for everyone" enabled, any team can guess it and take the point.
  • The first team to reach the target score wins. The round is always played to the end, and a tie adds one more round.

FAQ

Do I need an internet connection?
No. All word packs are built into the App, and the game works offline. A connection is needed only to buy or restore Alias Pro.
Can I continue an unfinished game?
Yes. An unfinished game is saved automatically and can be continued or deleted from the home screen.
What does Alias Pro include?
Additional word packs (movies, music, party, science, sport, hard, and the 18+ "After Midnight" pack), your own custom word packs, and new packs added in future updates. Pro is available as a monthly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase.
How do I create my own word pack?
With Alias Pro, open the word packs screen and create a new pack. A custom pack needs at least 10 words and can be edited or deleted at any time.
Is Alias suitable for kids?
Yes. There is a free kids pack for ages 6+. The "After Midnight" pack contains adult themes and is intended for players 18+ only.
How do I restore or cancel Pro?
Use "Restore Purchases" on the Pro screen to restore Pro on a new device. You can manage or cancel the subscription from the Pro screen or in your Apple Account subscription settings. Refunds are handled by Apple.
How do I reset statistics?
Open Settings and choose "Reset statistics". This cannot be undone.
How can I report a bug?
Email the device model, iOS version, app version, and steps that reproduce the issue.
