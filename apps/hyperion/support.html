FAQ

How do I control the ship?
Drag anywhere on the screen to fly around the tunnel wall. Double-tap to jump over barriers. Your ship fires automatically. In Settings you can switch steering to holding the left or right half of the screen and set the guns to fire only while your finger is down.
What is the difference between Endless and Sector Run?
In Endless the speed keeps growing until you crash, and your best score goes to the Game Center leaderboard. Sector Run is a 4 km route through all four sectors.
How do I restore my purchases?
Open the Store or Settings and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you made the purchases with.
Will my progress move to a new device?
Yes, when iCloud is turned on for Hyperion on both devices and they are signed in to the same Apple Account. The first sync can take a few minutes.
How do I remove ads?
Remove Ads or the Starter Pack turns off the full-screen ads between runs. Rewarded videos stay optional, and you watch them only when you choose to.
How do I turn on Pixel mode?
After you purchase Pixel mode it turns on automatically. You can switch it on or off at any time in Settings.
The game feels too fast or too slow to steer. What can I do?
Change the sensitivity in Settings. There you can also turn off jump barriers, reduce motion and glow, and turn off camera shake.
How do I change the game language?
Open Settings in the game and choose a language, or keep the automatic option to follow your device language.
How can I report a bug?
Email the device model, system version, app version, and the steps that reproduce the issue. A screenshot or a screen recording helps a lot.
