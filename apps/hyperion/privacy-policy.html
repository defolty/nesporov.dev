Privacy Policy - Hyperion

Last updated: October 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Hyperion ("the App") handles information when you use the App. Hyperion is an arcade game in which you fly a ship through a tunnel. The App does not require an account with the developer.

Game Progress

The App stores your game progress on your device: shards, unlocked ships, styles and upgrades, records, daily rewards, tasks, purchases you have made, and your settings. This information is used only to run the game.

iCloud Sync

If you are signed in to iCloud and iCloud is turned on for the App, your game progress is also stored in your private iCloud key-value storage and synchronized across your devices signed in to the same Apple Account. Apple processes this data under its own privacy policy. The developer cannot access your iCloud data. You can turn off iCloud for the App in your device settings.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, the App submits your best Endless score to the leaderboard and reports achievements. Your Game Center nickname and scores are visible to other players according to your Game Center settings. Game Center is provided by Apple under its own privacy policy.

In-App Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive your payment details. The App only receives a confirmation of which products you purchased, so it can unlock them and restore them on your other devices.

Advertising

The free version of the App shows ads through the Appodeal mediation platform: optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch for a reward, and full-screen ads between runs. Ads are never shown during a run. Purchasing Remove Ads or the Starter Pack turns off the full-screen ads.

Appodeal and the advertising networks it works with, such as Google AdMob, AppLovin, Unity Ads, ironSource, Mintegral, Meta Audience Network, Liftoff (Vungle), InMobi, DT Exchange and BidMachine, may collect and process information about your device and your interaction with ads. This can include the device's advertising identifier, IP address and the approximate location derived from it, device model, system version and language, and ad performance and diagnostic data. This information is used to deliver, measure and improve ads, to limit how often you see the same ad, and to prevent fraud.

The App asks for your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before the advertising identifier can be used. If you do not allow tracking, ads are shown without the advertising identifier. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the App asks for your consent through Google's consent form before ads are personalized, and you can change your choice at any time in the App's Settings. You can change your tracking permission at any time in your device settings under Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Learn more: Appodeal Privacy Policy https://appodeal.com/privacy-policy, and how Google uses information https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Analytics

The App does not include analytics SDKs. Apple may provide crash or diagnostic information if you have chosen to share it with developers in your device settings.

Data Sharing

The developer does not sell personal data and does not collect personal data on its own servers. Information is processed by the third parties described above only to provide their services.

Data Retention and Deletion

Game progress remains on your device until you remove the App. Progress synchronized through iCloud remains in your private iCloud storage until you remove the App's data from iCloud in your device settings. Data processed by Appodeal and its advertising partners is retained under their own policies.

Children's Privacy

Hyperion is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you have questions about a child's use of the App, please contact us.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your information. Because the developer does not hold your personal data, most requests are handled through your device settings, your Apple Account, Appodeal or its advertising partners. You can also contact us for help.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact: support@nesporov.dev
