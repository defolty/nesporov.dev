FAQ

Where is my data stored?
Cut Optimizer stores projects on your device by default. Pro users can enable iCloud sync in Settings to use the same projects on iPhone and iPad.
Which materials are supported?
Sheet materials such as plywood, chipboard, and MDF, linear stock such as timber and profiles, and roll materials such as fabric and film.
Does the App account for kerf, edge trimming, and grain?
Yes. Kerf, edge trim, grain direction, and edge banding thickness are set per project and used in every calculation.
How do I restore my purchase?
Open the paywall or the subscription screen in Settings and tap Restore. Use the same Apple Account that made the purchase.
How do I cancel my subscription?
Open Settings on your device, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Cut Optimizer. The subscription stays active until the end of the paid period.
How can I report a bug?
Email the device model, iOS version, app version, and steps that reproduce the issue. A screenshot of the project parameters helps a lot.
