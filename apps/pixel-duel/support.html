FAQ

How do I play with a friend?
Choose VS Friend, put the device flat between you, and each player drags a finger anywhere on their own half of the screen. Your paddle follows your finger.
Can I play alone?
Yes. Choose VS Bot and pick a difficulty. The bot plays the top half of the screen.
How do I change the match rules?
Before a match you can choose the arena, the points needed to win, the ball speed and whether power-ups are on.
How do I restore my purchases?
Open Settings and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account or Google account you made the purchases with.
How do I remove ads?
No Ads or the Starter Pack turns off the full-screen ads between matches. Rewarded videos stay optional, and you watch them only when you choose to.
How do I change my ad privacy choices?
Open Settings and tap Privacy Settings. The option appears where privacy laws apply to your region. On iOS you can also change tracking permission in your device settings under Privacy & Security > Tracking.
How do I change the game language?
Open Settings in the game and tap Language to switch between English and Russian.
How can I report a bug?
Email the device model, system version, app version, and the steps that reproduce the issue. A screenshot or a screen recording helps a lot.
