Privacy Policy - Pixel Duel

Last updated: October 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Pixel Duel ("the App") handles information when you use the App on iOS or Android. Pixel Duel is a pixel-art paddle game for two players on one screen or for one player against a bot. The App does not require an account with the developer.

Game Progress

The App stores your game progress on your device: coins, purchased and equipped paddles, balls and arenas, your last match setup, daily bonus and arena trial counters, whether you have seen the tutorial, purchases you have made, and your settings such as sound, music, vibration and language. This information is used only to run the game and is not sent to the developer.

In-App Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store or by Google through Google Play. The developer does not receive your payment details. The App only receives a confirmation of which products you purchased, so it can unlock them and restore them when you reinstall the App.

Advertising

The free version of the App shows ads through the Appodeal mediation platform: optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch, for example to try a premium arena or to double the coins from a match, and full-screen ads between matches. Ads are never shown during a match. Purchasing No Ads or the Starter Pack turns off the full-screen ads.

Appodeal and the advertising networks it works with, such as Google AdMob, AppLovin, Unity Ads, ironSource, Mintegral, Meta Audience Network, Liftoff (Vungle), InMobi, DT Exchange and BidMachine, may collect and process information about your device and your interaction with ads. This can include the device's advertising identifier, IP address and the approximate location derived from it, device model, system version and language, and ad performance and diagnostic data. This information is used to deliver, measure and improve ads, to limit how often you see the same ad, and to prevent fraud.

On iOS, the App asks for your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before the advertising identifier can be used. If you do not allow tracking, ads are shown without the advertising identifier. You can change this at any time in your device settings under Privacy & Security > Tracking. On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings under Privacy > Ads or Google > Ads.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the App asks for your consent through Google's consent form before ads are personalized. Residents of US states with privacy laws can opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for advertising. You can review or change your choice at any time in the App under Settings > Privacy Settings.

Learn more: Appodeal Privacy Policy https://appodeal.com/privacy-policy, and how Google uses information https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Analytics

The App does not include analytics SDKs. Apple or Google may provide crash or diagnostic information if you have chosen to share it with developers in your device settings.

Data Sharing

The developer does not sell personal data and does not collect personal data on its own servers. Information is processed by the third parties described above only to provide their services.

Data Retention and Deletion

Game progress remains on your device until you remove the App or clear its data. Data processed by Appodeal and its advertising partners is retained under their own policies.

Children's Privacy

Pixel Duel is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you have questions about a child's use of the App, please contact us.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your information. Because the developer does not hold your personal data, most requests are handled through the App's Privacy Settings, your device settings, your Apple or Google account, Appodeal or its advertising partners. You can also contact us for help.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact: support@nesporov.dev
