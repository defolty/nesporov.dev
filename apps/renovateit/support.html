FAQ

Where is my data stored?
On your device. If you turn on iCloud sync, projects, rooms, prices, and labor rates are also kept in your own private iCloud account. RenovateIt has no account and never uploads your data to the developer.
Which works can RenovateIt calculate?
Painting, priming, wallpaper, tiling, plastering, skim coat, drywall, insulation, laminate, underlay, baseboards, and screed. Materials include waste and are rounded up to whole packs.
Can I use my own prices and labor rates?
Yes, with RenovateIt Pro. You can set material prices with store names and your own labor rates, and reset them to defaults at any time.
How do I send an estimate to a client?
Open a project and choose "Export estimate PDF", then share it through Mail, Messages, AirDrop, Files, or any other app in the share sheet.
Does RenovateIt support imperial units and other currencies?
Yes. You can switch between metric and imperial units and choose your currency in Settings.
How do I sync between my iPhones?
With RenovateIt Pro, open Settings and turn on iCloud sync on each device. The devices must be signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud turned on. Changes can take a few moments to appear on the other device. Company details and the logo stay on each device.
What does RenovateIt Pro include?
Unlimited projects (the free plan includes 2), custom prices and labor rates, iCloud sync between your devices, your company logo on PDF estimates, and estimates without the "Made with RenovateIt" footer. Pro is available as a monthly or yearly subscription, or as a one-time lifetime purchase.
How do I restore or cancel Pro?
Use "Restore purchases" in Settings or on the Pro screen to restore Pro on a new device. You can manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple Account settings. Refunds are handled by Apple.
How can I report a bug?
Email the device model, iOS version, app version, and steps that reproduce the issue. Please remove client personal details from screenshots or attachments.
