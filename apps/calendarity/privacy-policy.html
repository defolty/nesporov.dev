Privacy Policy - Calendarity

Last updated: October 10, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Calendarity. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

By using the Calendarity app, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1. Information You Provide

We may collect the following information:

  • Username and email address during registration
  • Calendar data, events, and reminders you create
  • Notes and tasks added to the app
  • App settings and preferences

2.2. Automatically Collected Information

  • Device information (model, operating system, unique identifiers)
  • App usage data and feature interactions
  • IP address and connection information
  • Crash data and app performance metrics

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide and maintain app functionality
  • Sync your data across devices
  • Send notifications and reminders
  • Improve and personalize app experience
  • Analyze usage to improve our services
  • Ensure security and prevent fraud
  • Provide technical support
  • Comply with legal requirements

4. Data Storage

Your data is stored on secure servers using modern encryption methods. We implement the following security measures:

  • Data encryption in transit (SSL/TLS)
  • Data encryption at rest
  • Regular security audits
  • Limited access to personal data
  • Backup to prevent data loss

5. Third-Party Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal data to third parties. We may share data only in the following cases:

  • Service Providers: who help us operate the app (hosting, analytics)
  • Legal Requirements: if required to comply with legislation
  • With Your Consent: in other cases only with your explicit permission

6. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Access: request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Deletion: request deletion of your personal data
  • Export: receive your data in a structured format
  • Withdraw Consent: withdraw consent to data processing at any time
  • Object: object to certain types of processing

7. Cookies and Analytics

The app may use cookie-like technologies to improve user experience and collect analytics data. We use:

  • Analytics services to understand app usage
  • Error tracking tools to improve stability
  • Technologies for content personalization

You can manage analytics settings in the app settings section.

8. Children's Data

Our app is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and know that your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us.

9. International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed on servers located in different countries. We ensure that such transfers are carried out in accordance with applicable data protection laws and with appropriate security safeguards.

10. Data Retention Period

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary for the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy, or as required by law. After account deletion, your data will be deleted within 30 days.

11. Changes to Privacy Policy

We may periodically update our Privacy Policy. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date at the top.

We recommend periodically reviewing this Privacy Policy for updates. Changes become effective upon posting on this page.

12. Contact Information

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to exercise your rights, please contact us:

Email: support@nesporov.dev

13. Consent

By using our app, you agree to this Privacy Policy and the processing of your data in accordance with the terms set out in this document.
