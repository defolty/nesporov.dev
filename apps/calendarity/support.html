FAQ

How do I add my partner's schedule?
Adding a second user is part of Premium. Open Settings, get Premium, then set up the second user's name, colors, and shift pattern.
How do I change a single day?
Select a day, or several days at once, in the calendar and edit them manually. Manual edits are marked on the calendar and can be reset per user in Settings.
How do I remove ads?
Purchase Remove Ads or Premium Forever on the Premium screen. Premium Forever also unlocks the second user.
How do I restore my purchase?
Open the Premium screen and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account that made the purchase.
How can I report a bug?
Use Contact Developer in Settings or email us with the device model, iOS version, app version, and steps that reproduce the issue.
