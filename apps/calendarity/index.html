calendarity

Shared calendar for couples who work shifts. Put both schedules side by side and see at a glance when you can be together.

App Store ↗ features ↓
released · iPhone & iPad
// features

Built for partners with irregular hours — day shifts, night shifts, or any custom schedule.

Set up your shifts once, share them in one combined calendar, and stop guessing when you are both free.

  • 01

    Customizable shifts

    Define your schedule for every day of the month — day shifts, night shifts, or any hours — and edit it as plans change.
  • 02

    Shared calendar

    See your schedule and your partner's side by side in one unified calendar and quickly spot who works when.
  • 03

    Detailed statistics

    Breakdowns of hours and shifts for weekdays and weekends, with totals for each partner.
  • 04

    Schedule comparison

    Find the days you both work, or both have off, and highlight overlapping hours or free time.
  • 05

    Weekend planner

    See at a glance when you are both free on weekends — perfect for planning trips or simply resting together.
  • 06

    Simple by design

    A clean native interface: add or change shifts anytime and the calendar updates automatically.

For shift workers, healthcare professionals, parents, and anyone with changing schedules who wants to stay connected.
